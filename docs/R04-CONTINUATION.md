## Twenty additional research directions (all proposed)

1. **Grow an external nervous system:** construct actual delayed artificial sensor→actuator channels in the world; sever channels as a control.
2. **Build its own retina:** position lenses/occluders to improve otherwise ambiguous sensory discrimination.
3. **Outsource a reflex into matter:** optimize body stiffness/geometry until active correction is no longer necessary; attribute the optimization honestly.
4. **Define a body by control:** measure the world variables it can reliably sense and influence over a fixed horizon.
5. **Plant future laws:** deposit delayed, bounded local rule edits that change later interactions.
6. **Migrate between dimensions:** transport consistent local sensor/action contracts between graphs, 2D fields and 3D volumes.
7. **Make the world an address bus:** route packets through physical channels to select remote actuators.
8. **Share a skin, not a goal:** two agents with distinct objectives inhabit one constrained membrane.
9. **Reproduce construction programs:** offspring inherit rule grammars rather than only completed appearance.
10. **Recognize its work without labels:** no privileged provenance tags; use observations and past interactions, with swapped-history controls.
11. **Budget time as anatomy:** allocate a limited update budget across sensing, growth and action; avoid numerical artifacts.
12. **Eat causal power:** reward reproducible influence over future observations instead of sheer visual noise.
13. **Repair the feedback loop:** rebuilding channels restores sensing/behavioral capability, not merely shape.
14. **Negotiate sensory contracts:** agents with different encodings develop a limited shared signaling code.
15. **Build tools that alter interactions:** membranes/filters make previously unavailable material transformations possible.
16. **Inherit an ancestor's unfinished problem:** children inherit environmental deposits, restrictions and debts but not automatically learned policies.
17. **Grow out of a sense:** phase out one sensing pathway while enabling another.
18. **Pay for control infrastructure:** sensing, memory, communication and actuation incur explicit modeled resource costs.
19. **Branch biographies, not seeds:** identical saved-state copies undergo divergent developmental histories, then identical neutral probes.
20. **Cultivate dynamics it cannot command:** sustain and shape an autonomous mathematical pattern without controlling its every state.

### Nonnegotiable experimental structure taken from R04

**Sensor contract:** measured quantity, location/window, normalization, assigned cells, and which parts of the mapping are biological/inferred/invented.

**Action contract:** source neural populations, artificial controller contribution, what changes, material/energy cost, hard limits and feedback path.

**Learning contract:** changed parameters, training signal, hidden information exclusions, save/reset boundaries, and frozen evaluation procedure.

**Contribution ledger:** time, sensed state, stimulated neurons, recorded neural features, decoder output, local reflex/stochastic contributions, bounded executed action, cost, and measured subsequent effect.

**Two comparisons:** a frozen decoder with neural features disabled tests **dependence**; matched separately trained decoders on biological/rewired/non-neural features test **utility**. Neither supports a single universal “% fly controlled” score.
