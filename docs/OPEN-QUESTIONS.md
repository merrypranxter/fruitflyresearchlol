# Open questions, evidence conflicts & pitfalls

This is an active inquiry log, not a resolved scientific verdict.

1. **Which exact 6,203 neurons?** R01/R03 describe an escape-related circuit. Before attributing outputs to feeding, grooming, color, odor or mushroom-body learning, verify that those cells/pathways are *actually present*. Request a circuit manifest from the implementation.
2. **What generates spontaneous motion?** The simplified baseline Shiu-style circuit is often stimulus-driven; a hands-off creature will need a clear source of ongoing activity/exploration or an environment that produces stimuli. Log synthetic biases separately.
3. **Are the visual systems genuinely functional?** Direct downstream looming stimulation can work even when photoreceptor-level processing does not. Do not call an artificial looming value “natural visual perception.”
4. **Where does learning happen?** Small artificial readout adaptation is not biological synaptic learning; dopamine-like labels are not a validation protocol.
5. **Can rewired controls perform as well?** Compare acute disruption *and* matched retraining. A random network may serve the task adequately.
6. **How much interface is too much?** Trained body decoders can create sophisticated behavior despite biologically nonsensical cores (Digital Sphinx warning from R02). Limit/log adapter capacity and parameters.
7. **Does beauty require an agent?** Gorgeous math is allowed, but aesthetic output must be distinguished from claimed neural autonomy. Sometimes artistic merit and scientific causality should be evaluated separately.
8. **What is a body?** A controllable persistent process with constraints and feedback may be an adequate working definition; test no-body mathematical worlds against physical bodies.
9. **What makes an individual?** Identical deterministic initial states/histories do not magically diverge. Persist seeds, history, random noise, external reward and decoder weights.
10. **What is the correct mobile backend?** R01's optimistic WebGPU claims are not device benchmarks; R03 argues for conserving working JS/typed-array code, browser workers, measured memory, and hybrid CPU/GPU. Test before rewriting.
11. **What constitutes complete source capture?** Text extractions preserve words but mangle equations, tables, figures and some metadata. Preserve the original three PDFs in a separate upload, with hashes from the manifest.
12. **What belongs in the research repo?** Immutable source reports, syntheses, experiment protocols and manifest. Executable application code belongs in the implementation repo unless explicitly moved.
13. **Source disagreement/overstatement:** R01/R02/R03 include partially incompatible descriptions/benchmarks of community demos; treat each as attributed and check primary code/papers for any consequential claim.
14. **The incomplete invention atlas:** R03 terminates around #72 in the uploaded PDF. A continuation is expected, not yet archived.
15. **Provenance of generated reports:** These are user-provided deep-research outputs containing URLs and asserted findings. Do not assume that a listed URL is accessible, accurate or proof of its nearby statement.
