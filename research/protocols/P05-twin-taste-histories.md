# P05 — Twin Taste Histories

**Research source:** R04 pp. 30–34; [full text](../raw-text/R04-continuation-after-72.txt).
**Status:** design proposal.

**Question:** Can two identical starting organisms acquire durable experience-dependent behavioral differences rather than transient differences in needs or random parameters?

## Identical starting checkpoint
Freeze/copy connectome, neural equations, encoder, readout initialization, artificial drives, artistic prior, material reserves, and learning rule. Then give the twins **different environments** in which structured sparkle vs branching/filament construction is useful. Keep action costs and available affordances matched as far as possible.

## Distinguish state and learning locations
Log separately: (1) short-term reserves/damage/satiety, (2) acquired action value/skill, (3) any deliberately adjustable long-term preference coefficients. A new skill preference due to learned competence is not automatically an evolved intrinsic value.

## Neutral evaluation
After developmental histories: freeze all learning; equalize reserves and satiation; place both in a common arena; randomize action placement and superficial color; also change underlying mechanics while preserving appearance. Repeat across independent twin pairs.

## State swaps
Independently swap learned readouts, long-term preferences, reserves, neural transients and environmental history. Test which component carries behavioral differences; never assume “personality” resides in the biological graph.

## Controls
Frozen learner, shuffled reward, exploration without updates, identical histories, differing histories with equal material exposure, raw-sensor learner of equal capacity, rewired neural substrate.

## Measures and acceptance
Choices under neutral probes, retention after restart, generalization, reversal, stability, cost sensitivity and source of difference under state swaps. **Pass only when** divergence depends on history, persists after temporary-state equalization, survives neutral generalization and follows an identified learned component in swaps.

**Interpretation limit:** functional acquired preference, not aesthetic conscious experience.
